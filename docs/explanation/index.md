# Explanation

These pages tell why urn-forge works as it does. They do not give steps. For steps, use the [tutorials](../tutorials/) and the [how-to guides](../how-to/). For facts, use the [reference](../reference/).

- [The contract](./the-contract): what a domain contract is, how the manifest states it, and what it cannot state.
- [Core and shell](./core-and-shell): why handlers are pure, how brands prove a parse, and how a call succeeds or fails.
- [How urn-forge proves it](./how-urn-forge-proves-it): the chain from a URN to a scenario, what each gate shows, and why the output is always the same.
- [Compatibility and the direction of use](./compatibility): when a change to a URN breaks its consumers, and how a URN retires.
